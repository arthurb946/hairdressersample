A few words

Hair, looked after properly.

Nice to meet you. I'm Natalie — a fully insured, NVQ Level 3 hairstylist based in Shepperton, working full-time across Middlesex and Surrey.

For over two decades, I've been bringing the salon to my clients' kitchens, conservatories and living rooms. No waiting rooms, no rushed appointments, no upsell. Just thoughtful, careful work — and the time to do it right.

My customer base spans an eight-mile radius around Shepperton. Many of the people whose hair I cut today, I've been cutting for ten, fifteen, twenty years. Some of their children too.

— Natalie
